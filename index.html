Why These Four

Different categories. The same discipline.

A trading card storefront, an insurance agency, a construction firm, and a real estate practice don't share a supply chain — but they share an owner who reads every P&L, answers the phone, and is in the business, not just around it.

Sutika Capital exists to hold that ownership cleanly: proper entity structure, clear capital accounts, and enough distance to let each team run its own business well.
