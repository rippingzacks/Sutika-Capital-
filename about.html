About

A holding company run like an operating business.

Sutika Capital is the ownership structure behind a small group of businesses run by Matthew and Lainey Sutika — built to keep the paperwork clean and the operators focused.

The Approach

Every holding under Sutika Capital is chosen the same way: a category we understand, an operator who's already doing the work, and a structure clean enough that ownership never gets in the way of running the business.

That means proper entity formation, clear operating agreements between partners, and capital accounts that hold up under scrutiny — the unglamorous parts of a family business that matter most when something goes wrong.

We don't run a fund, and we're not raising outside capital. Sutika Capital is a private holding structure for businesses we own and operate ourselves.

The Principals

Matthew Sutika oversees the operations, technology, and legal/financial structuring across the portfolio — from e-commerce and web development to entity formation and capital agreements between partners.

Lainey Sutika is a Florida Realtor® specializing in new construction and luxury properties in Lakewood Ranch and The Villages, and co-owns Nimble Insurance and Agile Construction alongside Matthew.
